Risk classification guide

What each risk level means clinically, and how CardioSurv routes treatment decisions.

Low Risk
Stable profile
  • Asymptomatic or atypical chest pain
  • Normal resting ECG or mild ST changes
  • No exercise-induced angina
  • Flat or upsloping ST segment
BranchMedication
IntensityStandard / Low
2-yr Survival (est.)≥ 88%
Medium Risk
Elevated concern
  • Non-anginal or typical chest pain
  • ST-T abnormalities on ECG
  • Moderate cholesterol or blood pressure
  • Oldpeak 1.0 – 2.5
BranchMedication
IntensityModerate

High Risk
Critical intervention
  • Typical angina with exercise-induced symptoms
  • LVH on ECG, down-sloping ST segment
  • Oldpeak > 2.5, high GRACE score
  • Arrhythmia present → SBRT branch
